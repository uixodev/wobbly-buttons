What a project with me looks like, step by step

Every project starts with a chat and ends with a launch. Here is what happens in between, so nothing is a surprise.

1. Hello

We start with a conversation about you and your idea: who the website is for, what you want people to do on it, and what you like (and don’t like) about other sites. There’s no pressure and no jargon. Bring a rough idea; that’s plenty.

2. Sketch

Before anything is built, I sketch how the site could look and feel: the structure, the mood, the colours and the shapes. You see this early, so it’s easy to say “more of this, less of that”.

3. Build

Once we agree on the direction, I turn the sketch into a real website that works on phones, tablets and computers. You see it as it comes together, and we tweak it together along the way, not at the very end.

4. Launch

When you’re happy, I look after the launch: getting the site live on your own address and checking that everything works. You don’t need to figure out the technical side.

After launch

A website is never really “finished”. If you’d like, I can refresh an existing site later or look after it with ongoing support, so small changes don’t turn into small headaches.

What helps from you

  • A rough idea of what the site is for.
  • Any logo, photos or words you already have. If you don’t have them yet, we’ll work it out.
  • A few websites you like, and one or two you don’t.
