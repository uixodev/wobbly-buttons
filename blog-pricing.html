About my pricing

Every project is a bit different, so I quote after hearing about your idea. Here is how that works and what affects the price.

Why there’s no price list

A simple one-page site and a site with booking, a shop or an app are very different amounts of work. A fixed price list would either be too high for small projects or too low for big ones, so I quote each project after we talk.

What I promise

  • An honest range, once I’ve heard what you need.
  • No surprises. If something would change the price, I tell you before I do it.

What changes the price

  • How big the site is. A few pages or many.
  • What it needs to do. A simple contact form is one thing; bookings, a shop or an app is another.
  • The words and images. Whether you already have them, or need help creating them.
  • Creative extras. Custom illustrations and other hand-made details take more time.
  • Looking after it afterwards. A refresh or ongoing support is separate from the first build.

How to get a quote

Send me a short message about what you need, even if it’s only a rough idea. You can use the chat on the home page. I’ll come back with questions and an honest range.
